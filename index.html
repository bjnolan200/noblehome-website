The Residence

Move-in ready, in a neighborhood that has it all.

184 Oakland Avenue is a fully furnished duplex unit offering bright, comfortable space in Providence's Elmhurst neighborhood — minutes from Providence College and RIC, with an easy commute to the city's hospitals. Each unit comes completely turnkey: beds, sofas, dining set, smart TVs, and a fully equipped kitchen, so you can move in with just a suitcase. A shared washer and dryer are on-site in the building — a real convenience in Providence. Units are separately metered; tenants pay their own electric and gas, while the owner covers water and sewer. Flexible lease terms make this an ideal fit for graduate students, visiting faculty, and traveling healthcare professionals who want a quiet, secure, ready-to-live home.
