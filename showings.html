Income 3× the rent, credit 650+, no evictions in five years, and a background check before move-in. Pets welcome — flat $100/month.

The same standards apply to everyone, whether it is a one-month stay or a one-year lease:

  • Verifiable income of 3× the rent — a pay stub, offer or assignment letter, benefit award letter, or bank statements
  • Credit score of 650 or above
  • No eviction judgments in the past five years
  • A background check before move-in — everyone, every lease length

There is nothing to pay to book a viewing, and no application fee. If the room is right, we send the application link afterwards. It runs through Innago, where the screening company charges $35 for the credit and background check — paid to them, not to us, and only once you have seen the room. Already have a credit report and a Rhode Island BCI check from the last 90 days? Tell us first and we will use those instead, at no charge.
