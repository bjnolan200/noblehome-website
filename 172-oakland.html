The House

A restored 1920s farmhouse — rooms ready to move in.

172 Oakland Avenue — "The Roost" — is a lovingly restored historic farmhouse available for room-by-room rental. Eight private bedrooms each have keyed locks, plush bedding, blackout curtains, and a smart TV. The chef's kitchen features mahogany cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, a gas range, and a breakfast bar. A finished entertainment lounge with a foosball table and comfortable seating is ideal for movie nights or game-day watch parties. Central AC, washer/dryer on-site, and free parking for multiple cars. Each room is leased individually — ideal for students, travel nurses, professionals relocating to Providence, and anyone who wants a quality furnished room without the hassle of a whole apartment.

Questions & Answers

Everything people ask us.

Straight answers about the rooms, the money, and how paying rent works.

What does renting a room actually mean?
172 is a large house with eight private bedrooms. Each bedroom has a keyed lock and is leased separately — you sign for your room only, and you are not on the hook for anyone else's rent. Everyone shares one large kitchen, a formal dining room, the living space, the finished basement, and 2.5 bathrooms. When the house is full that is up to eight people sharing those spaces.
What do I need to pay to move in?
$1,300–$1,600 first month depending on the room, plus a $300 deposit and the $35 application fee paid to Innago. There is no broker fee and no last month's rent.
How much is the security deposit, and do I get it back?
$300 for a room, and it is refundable. Rhode Island law requires us to return it, with an itemized list of any deductions, within 20 days of you moving out.
Are utilities included?
Yes. Electricity, gas, water and WiFi are included in the rent. You do not put anything in your name and you never get a separate utility bill from us.
How long is the lease?
Month to month. Stay one month or stay for years. If you need to leave, give us notice and we will work with you — no long lock-in, which is the whole point of a furnished rental.
What furniture comes with it?
Everything. Bed, dresser, seating, a smart TV, and a kitchen stocked with cookware, dishes and appliances. Bring your clothes and your toothbrush.
Is there parking, and does it cost extra?
Parking is free and there is plenty of it. Spots are not assigned — first come, first parked.
Where do I do laundry?
There is a washer and dryer on site for residents. No laundromat trips, no coins.
Can I have pets?
Yes, with approval. There is a $100 per month pet fee. Tell us about your animal before you apply so there are no surprises.
Can I have guests stay over?
Of course. The only rule is that a guest is a guest: up to seven nights in a row. Anyone staying longer than that needs to be approved and added to the lease — that is how we keep the house fair for everyone paying rent.
Can I see it before I apply?
Yes, and you should. Looking is free. Only apply once you have seen the room and know you want it, because the $35 application fee is not refundable.
What does it cost to apply, and where does that money go?
The application costs $35, paid once, at the end. It goes to Innago, the software company that runs the credit, criminal and eviction check — not to Noble Home. We do not get any part of it, and it is not refunded if you are turned down. Your application does not reach us until that $35 is paid.
What do you look for in an application?
Steady income of roughly three times the rent, and no open evictions. We look at the whole picture, and we apply the same standard to everyone. If something on your record worries you, tell us up front — an honest explanation goes a long way, and finding out from the report instead of from you is what actually sinks applications.
Does bad credit or an eviction disqualify me?
Not automatically. Credit score alone does not decide it. What matters most is steady income and whether you have been straight with us.
What if I do not have a job?
That is fine. Social Security, SSI, disability, a pension, child support and benefits all count as income. There is a box on the application for "no employment history" — check it and enter your monthly income.
Do I really have to give my Social Security Number?
Yes — the application will not go through without it, because that is how the background check is run. Innago handles that, not us. We never see your full number; we only see the finished report.
How do I pay rent once I move in?
Through Innago, online or in the app. Paying from a bank account costs $2. Paying by card costs 2.99% — about $39 a month on a $1,300 room, which is over $450 a year. Use the bank option if you possibly can, and turn on autopay the day you sign.
What happens if I pay late, or can only pay part?
Rent is late after a two-day grace period, and the system adds a $100 late fee automatically — nobody adds it by hand and nobody can wave it off. Innago does accept partial payments, so pay what you can and tell us before the due date. We would much rather work something out than chase you.
Can I pay with Venmo, Cash App, or cash?
Please do not. Pay through Innago so there is a record of every payment. That record protects you as much as it protects us.
Something broke. How do I tell you?
Send a maintenance request in the Innago app — it goes straight to us with photos and you can see the status. Or call Jen at (401) 829-6011.
